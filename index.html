Our story

From fibre to cloth,
made in Peru.

Heavy fabrics and throws in baby alpaca and Pima cotton.

The fibre

Two natural fibres, one yarn.

Peru is the home of the alpaca. We make the cloth close to where the fibre comes from.

  • Baby alpaca60% of the blend. One of the finest grades of alpaca fibre, in natural shades from ivory to deep brown.
  • Pima cotton40% of the blend. One of the finest long-staple cottons grown anywhere, knitted into the tube that holds the alpaca.
The yarn

Baby alpaca, blown into Pima cotton.

Our signature is a blown yarn. Baby alpaca is air-blown into a fine knitted tube of Pima cotton, so the yarn is 100% natural fibre, with no nylon at its core. The air held between the fibres gives the cloth volume and warmth, and keeps it light in the hand.
